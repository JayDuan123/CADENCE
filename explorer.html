    About this data. The 1,000 atoms are the random sample of interpretable atoms used for the paper’s describe-then-validate run (gpt-5.4-mini). For each atom, the three held-out MIMIC-IV-ECG test recordings with the highest activation are shown, in the lead where the atom fires most; red marks the 100 ms patches where it is active, and deeper red means stronger activation. Activations are recomputed with the frozen CSFM backbone and the Layer-6 SAE. “Best phenotype” is the single diagnostic label the atom separates best (AUROC). LLM ρ is the Spearman correlation between activations the LLM predicted from the description alone and the measured ones on held-out crops.

    ECG waveforms: MIMIC-IV-ECG (Gow et al., PhysioNet), Open Data Commons Open Database License v1.0. Not for clinical use.
